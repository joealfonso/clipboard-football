# RULES.md

The NFL rules the sim implements. Each rule cites a current source (rulebook section or official NFL operations page, with the season it applies to). Rules are looked up, never written from memory.

## How to add a rule

Entry format: the rule in plain words, how the sim applies it, source link, season, and date checked.

## Kickoffs

The current kickoff format and touchback rules.

## Scoring and tries

Touchdowns, field goals, safeties, extra points and two-point tries.

## Clock

Game clock, play clock, two-minute warning and clock stoppages.

## Overtime

Regular season and postseason overtime procedure.

## Timeouts

How many each team has and when they can be used.

## Challenges and replay review

Coach's challenges and booth reviews, if the game models them.

## Penalties

Each penalty the sim can call, its yardage, and whether it carries an automatic first down or loss of down.
