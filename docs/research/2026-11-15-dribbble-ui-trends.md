# Dribbble UI trends, 2026-11-15

Research for the "improve the UI" pass Joseph asked for. Sources: Dribbble searches "football game ui", "sports dashboard dark", "nfl scoreboard" (viewed in the browser on 2026-11-15), and the daisyUI trends page on "sports brutalism" (https://trends.daisyui.com/trend/sports-brutalism/). Observations, not a formal trend report.

## What recurs

1. **Near-black stage, one electric accent.** The most-viewed football game concepts (Flatstudio's game UI series) sit on #0B0B0C-ish black and charcoal, not navy, with a single acid lime/yellow accent used only for the primary action ("START MATCH") and the live selection. Everything else is white at stepped opacities.
2. **Bento grid dashboards.** Sports dashboards (Digcy, Orbix, Cansaas, Awsmd) tile the screen with cards of different sizes: one hero card (big score, player, or match), then small stat tiles, a mini chart, a list. No large empty regions. Rounded 16 to 24 px corners, 8 to 12 px gutters.
3. **Oversized condensed numerals.** Scores and key stats are huge (the "24 | 17 FINAL" poster style), condensed, tabular. Labels are tiny uppercase with wide tracking.
4. **Console HUD chrome.** Game UIs frame the screen with tiny uppercase corner labels (screen name top left, mode top right) and a full-width bottom key-hint bar (SELECT, BACK, HELP) with keycap chips.
5. **Pill and segmented controls.** Bottom action bars of pills, with the primary pill filled in the accent color and the rest dark.
6. **Hard team-color blocks and split screens.** Matchups are two halves in team color, sometimes slanted. TV score bugs use solid team plates.
7. **Texture, lightly.** Subtle grain/noise and halftone on hero areas, never on reading surfaces.
8. **Motion:** score flips, counters rolling up, hard wipes on decisive moments.

## What the current build looks like against that (screenshots taken 2026-11-15 at 1440x900)

- Main menu: title, four rows and a small play loop card occupy the middle third; the rest of the screen is empty navy.
- Calling screen: situation plate across the top (good), then "Last play" card (left) and the menu card (right), and roughly the bottom 45% of the screen is empty. Rows are all the same weight. The only accent is gold.
- Coin toss: a big header card with one word, two near-empty cards.
- Play screen: field fills the center column, the right rail has two small cards and lots of empty stage.
- Everything is navy on navy; cards blend into the stage.
